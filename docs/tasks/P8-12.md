### Findings (0 blocker, 4 major, 10 minor)

- **Major 1-2:** the mid-payload truncation arms of the geometry
  curve readers (`shape.rs` 519-549, 1342-1343, 1653-1785) and of the
  text row readers (`text.rs` 379-428, 623-642, 719-751, 850-864,
  903-943, 1881-1940) are untested: the sweeps truncate at record
  boundaries, not inside a field. Needs a payload-level truncation
  sweep per record kind; `shape.rs` is also where P8-15 open issue 2
  (the duplicated vertices) lives.
- **Major 3:** the open path's error arms are half dark (`file.rs`
  444 `read_input_document`, 288 `to_model`, 538 `encoded`, 770
  `write_file`): a corrupt picked file, a corrupt package and a
  failing write have no test. One test per arm.
- **Major 4:** the `WarningKind`/`DocumentKind` catch-alls
  (`file.rs` 265, 877, 917, 949) are unreachable today (foreign
  `#[non_exhaustive]` enums) but will silently rot; `WarningKind`'s
  `Display` (P8-09's open issue) removes the mapping.
- **Minor:** the `shape.rs` stale guards (1077, 1117 and the arms at
  1102, 1113); the hostile date-field arms (`text.rs` 1194-1195,
  1297); defensive checked arithmetic behind length pre-checks
  (`chunks.rs`, `container.rs`, `style.rs`, `pages.rs`,
  `pipeline.rs`); the CLI export/render error arms (`file.rs`
  723-770); test-only dark arms (corpus-skip, panic else-arms);
  `parse.rs` 312; the native picker wiring with P8-09's dialog
  filters (`dialogs.rs` 130-143, 175-189; carried P7-12 finding 2);
  the oracle-gated parity/twin/wide runs never in the gate; the
  render goldens outside the line table; the carried app-shell and
  `shell.rs` gaps; `coverage.toml`'s dead `xtask` ratchet key.
- **Recommendation flags:** findings 1-3 and the date-field arm need
  tests; the catch-alls and the stale guards are unreachable or
  defensive (comment or delete); the checked-arithmetic arms should
  be verified per arm and marked, not tested one by one.
