## Goal

0. **A picker click deselects the shape** (user's second pass,
   2026-10-31, at `514e45b`): a click on a Fill or Line swatch or a
   transparency row deselects the shape and changes nothing. The click
   most likely reaches the canvas under the popup. Fix the root cause:
   the canvas ignores a press that lands on a popup, menu or other area
   above it. This is probably why items 1 and 2 looked dead. The fix
   may touch the canvas's pointer path in `crates/ui/src/canvas.rs`
   (P7-22 owns the keyboard and right-click paths there; keep the hunk
   small and rebase).
1. **Formatting works from the selection** in both layouts: with one
   or more shapes selected, fill colour, line colour, line weight,
   line pattern and arrowheads are enabled and apply from the Format
   menu, the formatting toolbar (Panel Classic) and the Home tab
   (Panel Modern). With nothing selected they are disabled, with the
   reason in the tooltip.
2. **Font and size** are enabled with a shape or text selected
   (including right after adding text) and apply. Panel Classic's font
   and size boxes get drop-down lists, like Panel Modern's (P7-21's
   open issue).
3. **The Zoom box** in Panel Classic's toolbar: typing a value and
   pressing Enter sets the zoom (`shape.draw.zoom` with `percent`), and
   its drop-down offers the presets.
4. and 5. Moved to P7-25.
6. **Format Painter on the canvas:** after the button, a click on a
   shape applies the copied format (P7-21's open issue), and Escape
   cancels.
7. **Transparency rows** show a small preview swatch (the fill colour
   over a checkerboard at that transparency) beside the percentage, and
   choosing one applies.
8. and 9. Moved to P7-25, except one part of item 8 that stays here:
   the text controls (font, size, Bold, Italic, Underline, alignment)
   stay enabled on a shape without text, and the format applies to text
   typed later.
10. **The toggle buttons show the current state:** with a text object
   or shape selected, Bold, Italic, Underline and the alignment buttons
   show which setting is on, in both layouts.
11. **Page Setup** reads the page size from the page as rendered,
   including a size inherited from a background page or style (P7-21's
   open issue).
