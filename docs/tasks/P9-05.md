## Goal

1. **Regions in the renderer.** `render_png_region(scene, options,
   region)` and `render_svg_region(scene, options, region)`: the
   rectangle (inches, page coordinates) at the options' scale, its
   bottom left at the image's bottom left, everything outside clipped.
   The region is checked (finite, positive, inside the page) and the
   pixmap size is checked against the renderer's existing caps before
   allocation. Deterministic output, as ADR 0025 requires.
2. **`control::render`.** `parse_background`, `image_size` (every check
   of ADR 0079 before any allocation, the `LimitExceeded` values in
   pixels), `render_page` (build the scene, choose the full-page or
   region path, the format, the background).
3. **Tests.** In `crates/render`: a region of a hand-built scene
   matches the same area of the full render pixel for pixel (PNG) and
   clips correctly (SVG); a bad region errors. In `crates/control`:
   every `image_size` refusal, both formats, the three backgrounds, a
   region, and a request one pixel over each cap.
