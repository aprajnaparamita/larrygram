## The user's findings (2026-10-26)

- In the Shapes window, only a master's label is clickable; the whole
  tile (picture and label) must select and drag.
- The borders between areas that can be dragged to resize (the panes
  and the canvas) do not resize reliably.
- The UI is not usable even for basic testing: it must feel polished.

## Goal

1. **Shapes window**: the whole tile is the hit target for hover,
   click, double-click and drag (drag a master onto the page); hover
   and selected states are visible; keyboard selection (arrow keys,
   Enter to drop at the page centre) works; the stencil headers
   collapse and expand on the whole header row. Search filters the
   tiles.
2. **Splitters**: every pane border (Shapes window, Shape Data and the
   other side panes, the page tabs and status bar if resizable)
   resizes reliably: a hit zone of at least 6 px, the resize cursor on
   hover, no jitter, minimum and maximum widths, a double-click that
   restores the default width, and the widths remembered in the
   settings across restarts. Panes collapse and reopen without losing
   their width.
3. **Shape handles on the canvas**: the selection, resize and rotation
   handles are hit reliably at every zoom and at display scales 1 and
   2 (hit zones in screen pixels, not page units); the cursor shows the
   operation; a click on a handle never starts a marquee.
4. **Tests**: kittest drag tests for each of the above (a tile dragged
   onto the page creates the shape; a splitter drag changes the width
   by the drag distance within the limits; a handle drag at 25%, 100%
   and 400% zoom resizes the shape), at both display scales.
5. **Report** what you changed per surface, before and after, in the
   Result.
