## The user's findings (2026-10-26)

- In the Shapes window, only a master's label is clickable; the whole
  tile (picture and label) must select and drag.
- The borders between areas that can be dragged to resize (the panes
  and the canvas) do not resize reliably.
- The UI is not usable even for basic testing: it must feel polished.

## Goal

1. **Shapes window**: the whole tile is the hit target for hover,
   click, double-click and drag (drag a master onto the page); hover
   and selected states are visible; keyboard selection (arrow keys,
   Enter to drop at the page centre) works; the stencil headers
   collapse and expand on the whole header row. Search filters the
   tiles.
2. **Splitters**: every pane border (Shapes window, Shape Data and the
   other side panes, the page tabs and status bar if resizable)
   resizes reliably: a hit zone of at least 6 px, the resize cursor on
   hover, no jitter, minimum and maximum widths, a double-click that
   restores the default width, and the widths remembered in the
   settings across restarts. Panes collapse and reopen without losing
   their width.
3. **Shape handles on the canvas**: the selection, resize and rotation
   handles are hit reliably at every zoom and at display scales 1 and
   2 (hit zones in screen pixels, not page units); the cursor shows the
   operation; a click on a handle never starts a marquee.
4. **Tests**: kittest drag tests for each of the above (a tile dragged
   onto the page creates the shape; a splitter drag changes the width
   by the drag distance within the limits; a handle drag at 25%, 100%
   and 400% zoom resizes the shape), at both display scales.
5. **Report** what you changed per surface, before and after, in the
   Result.

## Result

**What changed, per surface (before → after)**

- **Shapes window** (`shapes_window.rs`). Before: each master was a
  preview image beside a `Button::selectable` label; only the label
  took clicks and drags, the stencil sections were egui collapsing
  headers (arrow and text only), the search box did nothing, there were
  no keys and the list did not scroll. After: one tile per master
  (preview and label in one `Sense::CLICK | DRAG` rect, not focusable)
  with hover (`button.hover.*`) and selected (`button.pressed.*`) looks
  and grab cursors; click selects, double-click drops at the page
  centre, drag starts the stencil drag. The whole header row toggles
  its section. The list scrolls. The search box filters as you type
  (name or `NameU`, case-insensitive; matching sections open; "No
  shapes match"). While the window has the keyboard (a press in it;
  a press elsewhere takes it away) Up/Down/Home/End move the selection
  and Enter drops it at the page centre; the shell takes these keys
  before the shortcut table, so the arrows no longer nudge shapes then.
  The Classic close button collapses the window (it was a disabled
  stub); the dead "Go" button went. Design follow-ups: the Modern title
  uses the `strong` role (was a fixed 22 px Light), "Stencils" and
  "Search" in title case, the placeholder outline reads `chrome.border`.
  Drops go through `insert.shape` (one undo step).
- **Splitters** (new `splitter.rs`; `shell.rs` layout). Before: egui's
  panel resize zone straddled the edge and the canvas, registered after
  the panel, took the half over the drawing (about 3 pt left), the
  minimum width equalled the default (panes could only grow) and
  nothing persisted. After: one `Splitter` per border: the Shapes window,
  the right-hand panes, and a new one between Shape Data and Size &
  Position (each pane now in its own scroll area). Its 8 pt zone is
  registered after the canvas, so it wins on both sides of the edge; a
  press on it never reaches the canvas (no marquee). The size follows
  the drag distance from the press within the limits (min 120 pt,
  max 45% of the window; heights min 60 pt); the resize cursor and an
  `accent` line show on hover and drag; a double-click restores the
  default; a drag past half the minimum collapses the pane to a 10 pt
  strip that reopens at the pre-drag width on a click. Sizes and
  collapse persist in `Settings::pane_sizes` (written once per gesture)
  and come back on restart. The page tabs and status bar stay fixed
  height (not resizable).
- **Handles** (`handles.rs`, `canvas.rs`, `tools/pointer.rs`). Before:
  the rotate handle sat a fixed 0.25 in above the shape (6 px at 25%
  zoom, inside the north handle's reach, so the north handle won; an
  inch away at 400%), the first handle within 6 px won rather than the
  nearest, the move interior of a turned shape was its axis-aligned
  envelope, no cursor showed the operation, the press position was the
  pointer's position at the end of the press frame, and a Shift or Ctrl
  press on a handle outside the shape started a marquee. After: the
  rotate handle is 20 screen px above the top edge at every zoom
  (`ROTATE_OFFSET_PX`, `handles_for` takes the zoom) and draws round;
  hit zones are in screen points (8 pt squares, `HANDLE_HALF_PX`, always
  hit; outside the shape a 7 px reach), the nearest handle wins, a small
  shape keeps a move interior and the interior test is exact for turned
  shapes; the cursor shows move, the resize direction (turned with the
  shape) or grab/grabbing for rotate, and stays for the whole drag; the
  press lands at the press origin; a resize or rotate handle wins with
  any modifier.
- **Canvas** (`canvas.rs`, your follow-ups): grid, selection, handle,
  snap-guide and marquee colours read theme tokens (closest existing
  ones, see Open issues); the canvas records `ViewState::viewport` each
  frame, so Fit to Window, Fit page and Fit width run without asking.
- **Shell** (`shell.rs`, your follow-ups): full screen
  (`AppCtx::full_screen`) draws the drawing alone and sends
  `ViewportCommand::Fullscreen`; Esc leaves full screen but no longer
  enters it (the shortcut table binds Esc and F5 to the same toggle);
  the Options window calls `theme::raise_controls`; the status-bar hover
  and press fills read `button.hover.fill` and `button.active.fill`;
  "PAGE 1 OF 1" is now "Page 1 of 1".
- **Shape Data pane** (`panes.rs`): the value fields get the
  `control.border` edge through `raise_controls`.

**Tests.** `crates/ui/tests/phase7/interaction.rs` (new, 13 kittest
tests through the real shell, every one at display scales 1 and 2
