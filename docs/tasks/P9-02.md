## Goal

1. **`jsonrpc.rs`.** `parse_message`: UTF-8 JSON, the depth cap
   checked before serde recurses (a cheap pre-scan of the bytes is
   fine), batches and responses refused, every malformed shape a
   `Rejected` that keeps the id when it can be read.
   `encode_response`, `encode_request`, `encode_notification`,
   `parse_response`; a result over `max_response_bytes` becomes a
   `LimitExceeded` error response for the same id.
2. **`framing.rs`.** `FrameReader::read_frame` with the cap enforced
   while reading (never buffer more than the cap plus one read),
   discard to the newline on overflow, skip empty lines, accept a last
   line without a newline, drop a trailing `\r`. `write_frame`.
3. **`serve.rs`.** `serve`: the loop of ADR 0075 (read, parse, admit,
   handle, write), `FrameError` and `Rejected` answered without ending
   the loop, `ServeEnd::Eof` at end of stream, `ServeEnd::Closed` when
   the front end asks. `RateLimiter::admit` as a token bucket (the
   loop passes `Instant::now()`; tests pass their own instants).
4. **`protocol.rs`.** `negotiate`, `decode_op`, `encode_op`,
   `encode_result`, `decode_result` (the method table is `Op::METHODS`;
   params structs deserialize with `deny_unknown_fields`), and the
   `ControlServer` front end: the `hello` handshake and token check of
   ADR 0075 (a wrong token answers `Unauthorized` and sets
   `close_after`), `server/discover` and every operation forwarded to
   the backend, notifications ignored.
5. **Tests.** Every refusal of `parse_message` and every framing edge
   case; a transcript test (requests in, responses out) of `serve` with
   `ControlServer` over a fake `Backend` that returns canned results;
   `encode_op`/`decode_op` and `encode_result`/`decode_result` round
   trips for every op; the rate limiter with fixed instants.

Tests must not depend on another wave A task's stub (see the plan,
section 4): use your own fake backend.
