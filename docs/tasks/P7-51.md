## Why

The Opus review of P7-48 (2026-11-04) found no blockers and one major;
P7-41 and P7-48 merged with this follow-up.

- **Major:** the field ignores the text block's margins and alignment.
  It sits at the block's top-left with no margin, left-aligned, and
  wraps at the full block width. The drawn text sits inside
  `LeftMargin`/`RightMargin` (4 pt by default) and, for our masters,
  centred (P7-44). With the drawn text hidden while editing (P7-48
  item 2), a centred label jumps to the left edge when editing starts
  and back on commit, and line breaks can change on commit (the field
  wraps about 11 px wider at 1×).
- Minors 1, 2 and 6, and the small ones (3, 4, 5, 7, 8).

## Goal

1. **The field sits where the text is drawn** (the major). Inset the
   field by the block's text margins, wrap at the inner width, and set
   its horizontal alignment from the first paragraph's `HorzAlign`
   (left, centre, right; justified and others as left). Keep the
   block joined with the field's rect as the commit test (P7-48 item
   1). Vertical placement keeps the first baseline as today. Tests,
   both layouts: on a centred master, the typed text's ink is centred
   in the block within a pixel or two at 1× and 2×; a line that wraps
   when drawn wraps at the same word in the field; a shape with
   `LeftMargin` 0.5 in starts its field 0.5 in from the block's left
   edge. Regenerate the two editor goldens and say in the Result how
   they changed.
2. **The empty-box flag is fresh** (minor 1). `pending_text_empty` is
   refreshed after the editor has taken the frame's keystrokes (at the
   end of `show_in`, or wherever runs before `publish_changed`). Test:
   on a clean drawing, after the first typed character, the same frame
   reads as changed (the title marker and the published flag).
3. **One keep-the-edit area** (minor 2). A Text-tool press inside the
   edited shape's text block, but off the field, keeps the edit and
   moves the caret to the nearest position, with no new undo step.
   Test with real input: in an existing shape's block, click below the
   one-row field, then type; the text lands in the edit and the undo
   list has one step. Change the P7-25 click test so it proves the
   caret moved: click mid-word, type, check where the letter landed.
4. **Hit-testing a text block** (minor 6). P7-48's `TextNode::block`
   made hit-testing and bounds use the whole text block, not the
   content-sized box at the block's corner. Add one test in
   `crates/render/tests/hit.rs` where the block is larger than the
   laid-out text: a press inside the block but outside the text hits
   the shape, and the bounds are the block's. Say the behaviour change
   in the Result.
5. **The small ones:**

## Result

Done (2026-11-04, `agent/P7-51`, on top of `main` at `7d4f332`). Items
1 to 5:

1. **The field sits where the text is drawn (the major).**
   `TextNode` carries the block's text margins and the first
   paragraph's `HorzAlign` (filled where `block` is filled in
   `build.rs`), and the field follows them: inset by the margins,
   wrapping at the inner width, aligned left, centre or right
   (justified and unknown alignments edit left). The block joined
   with the field's rect stays the commit test; the first-baseline
   rule is unchanged. egui does the alignment through
   `TextEdit::align`, whose layouter aligns each row like the drawn
   paragraph. Tests, real input, both layouts: on a centred master
   the typed text's ink is centred in the block within 2 px at 1× and
   2×; a line that wraps when drawn wraps at the same word in the
   field (the same face, "Selawik", on both sides); a shape with
   `LeftMargin` 0.5 in starts its field 48 px (±3) from the block's
   left edge. Unit tests pin the inset and the alignment mapping,
   and the fallback to the block when the margins eat it.
2. **The empty-box flag is fresh.** `pending_text_empty` refreshes at
   the end of `show_in` (after the editor took the frame's
   keystrokes, before `publish_changed` reads `is_dirty`), and the
   window title syncs after it. The test types the first character
   in exactly one frame (`run_steps(1)`) on a clean drawing and reads
   the title marker and the published flag the same frame (it fails
   without the end-of-frame refresh).
3. **One keep-the-edit area.** A press inside the edited shape's
   text block but off the field keeps the edit and moves the caret to
   the nearest position: `text_edit::show` moves the caret (the
   field's galley maps the press to its nearest cursor) and the
   field re-takes the keyboard on the next frame (egui surrenders a
   field's focus one frame after a press outside it — the P7-41
   refocus covers it). The shell's Text-tool rule drops such presses
   (`TextEditor::press_keeps_edit`, read from where the edit drew
   last frame), so no commit, no box over the block and no new undo
   step. The P7-25 click test now proves the caret moved (a mid-word
   click lands the letter at the click: "MM" + click + "X" reads
   "MMXMM"); a new test clicks below the one-row field of an existing
   shape's block and checks the typing lands in the edit with one
   undo step.
4. **Hit-testing a text block.** One test in
   `crates/render/tests/hit.rs` where the block is larger than the
   laid-out text: a press inside the block but outside the text hits
   the shape, and `node_bounds` answers the block's rect.
   **Behaviour change pinned (from P7-48):** hit-testing and bounds
   use the whole text block (`TextNode::block`), not the
   content-sized box at the block's corner.
5. **The small ones.** The "Can't Redo, disabled" check undoes one
   step first so a redo tail exists (minor 3); the Panel Modern Undo
   history list lists the pending step first while a non-empty group
   is open, matching the button's label (minor 4); the Size field's
   buffer is a per-picker, per-popup draft — cleared when its popup
   closes without a choice and never shared between size pickers
   (minor 5, one unit test per half plus the real-popup flow); the
   stale P7-25 commit-first comment says what the code does now
   (minor 7, landed with item 3); the Bold and 36 pt picture checks
   compare against the plain picture (the format must visibly change
   the picture) and the M2 test checks the text comes back after a
   `text.cancel` (minor 8).
