## What is wrong today (master review, 2026-10-25)

Every corpus `.vsd` renders badly next to the reference:

- `dwg`: positions and outlines right, but no fill, and an arrowhead
  on every outline (a line-end value read where there is none).
- `fdo86729-*`: the text "aaa" above the top shape is missing; no fill.
- `no-bgcolor`: the group's text lands at the bottom left of the page
  and the box outline is missing (child shapes placed without their
  group's transform).
- `Visio11FormatLine`: no lines at all (1-D shapes draw nothing); the
  labels wrap far too narrow (text box width).
- `tdf76829-datetime-format`: no fills, wrong font sizes; its second
  page is never rendered (goldens are first page only).
- `Visio6PlanWithDimensions` (a scaled drawing, feet on a letter page):
  nothing recognisable; the page background colour, the filled room
  and the dimension labels are missing.

## Goal

1. **Style resolution.** Line, fill and line-end values reach the
   shapes through their styles and masters as Visio resolves them: no
   fill where there is one, no arrowhead where there is none.
2. **1-D shapes.** Lines and connectors draw (their geometry and
   begin/end points).
3. **Groups.** Child shapes are placed in their group's coordinate
   system; the group's own geometry and text draw.
4. **Scaled drawings.** Drawings with a drawing scale render at page
   scale (`PageScale`/`DrawingScale`, as the `.vsdx` reader treats the
   same cells).
5. **Text.** Missing text appears; font sizes and text box widths come
   from the right cells.
6. **Page background colour** where the file has one.
7. **Every page.** The goldens and the corpus tests cover every page
   of every file, not only the first.
8. **The twin comparison** (once the twins are present).
   `tests/twins.rs` compares `import(<name>.vsd)` with the `.vsdx`
   reader's document for `<name>.vsdx`:
   - pages, shapes, the cells the renderer reads, and text, per page;
   - the twins are found through `LARRYGRAM_VSD_TWINS` (the directory
     that holds `vsdx/`);
   - the test skips cleanly when the variable is unset, like the
     libvisio oracle.

   The repo calls them "the `.vsdx` twins of the corpus files" and
   says nothing more about where they come from. Commit only derived
   facts (expected values, tolerances), never a twin or a PNG.
