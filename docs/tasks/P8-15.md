## What is wrong today (master review, 2026-10-25)

Every corpus `.vsd` renders badly next to the reference:

- `dwg`: positions and outlines right, but no fill, and an arrowhead
  on every outline (a line-end value read where there is none).
- `fdo86729-*`: the text "aaa" above the top shape is missing; no fill.
- `no-bgcolor`: the group's text lands at the bottom left of the page
  and the box outline is missing (child shapes placed without their
  group's transform).
- `Visio11FormatLine`: no lines at all (1-D shapes draw nothing); the
  labels wrap far too narrow (text box width).
- `tdf76829-datetime-format`: no fills, wrong font sizes; its second
  page is never rendered (goldens are first page only).
- `Visio6PlanWithDimensions` (a scaled drawing, feet on a letter page):
  nothing recognisable; the page background colour, the filled room
  and the dimension labels are missing.

## Goal

1. **Style resolution.** Line, fill and line-end values reach the
   shapes through their styles and masters as Visio resolves them: no
   fill where there is one, no arrowhead where there is none.
2. **1-D shapes.** Lines and connectors draw (their geometry and
   begin/end points).
3. **Groups.** Child shapes are placed in their group's coordinate
   system; the group's own geometry and text draw.
4. **Scaled drawings.** Drawings with a drawing scale render at page
   scale (`PageScale`/`DrawingScale`, as the `.vsdx` reader treats the
   same cells).
5. **Text.** Missing text appears; font sizes and text box widths come
   from the right cells.
6. **Page background colour** where the file has one.
7. **Every page.** The goldens and the corpus tests cover every page
   of every file, not only the first.
8. **The twin comparison** (once the twins are present).
   `tests/twins.rs` compares `import(<name>.vsd)` with the `.vsdx`
   reader's document for `<name>.vsdx`:
   - pages, shapes, the cells the renderer reads, and text, per page;
   - the twins are found through `LARRYGRAM_VSD_TWINS` (the directory
     that holds `vsdx/`);
   - the test skips cleanly when the variable is unset, like the
     libvisio oracle.

   The repo calls them "the `.vsdx` twins of the corpus files" and
   says nothing more about where they come from. Commit only derived
   facts (expected values, tolerances), never a twin or a PNG.

### What was built

1. **Style resolution** (goal 1). A shape record group's own `Line`
   (`0x85`), `FillAndShadow` (`0x86`) and `TextBlock` (`0x87`) rows now
   map onto the shape (P8-10's open issue 2); they were dropped, so
   every instance fell back to the hard defaults. The line record's
   arrow and cap bytes are read after the rounding value's format byte
   (25, 26, 27, the reference's offsets): reading them at 24 put a
   begin arrow on every outline (`dwg.vsd`) and lost the cap.
2. **1-D shapes** (goal 2). They drew nothing because the line
   properties never reached them; with the rows mapped, the line
   samples of `Visio11FormatLine.vsd` draw with their weights, corners
   and arrowheads.
3. **Groups** (goal 3). A shape takes its Visio id from its record id
   (the shape list names the same ids in z-order; the old mapping
   assigned the list payloads in stream order, so ids and names landed
   on the wrong shapes), and the shape record's parent field joins a
   group to its sub-shapes, which are its siblings in the stream. The
   shape lists give the paint order (their trailer, not the record
   order): `Visio6PlanWithDimensions.vsd`'s room fill was painted over
   its own walls.
4. **Scaled drawings** (goal 4). `src/scale.rs` applies the page's
   `PageScale`/`DrawingScale` to every length of its shapes (the page
   size was already scaled by P8-10), and to the masters and styles
   with the first page's scale. Angles, patterns, point sizes and the
   formula-encoded `PolylineTo`/`NurbsTo` rows do not scale.
5. **Text** (goal 5). The text joins to its shape by record id now (the
   `fdo86729-*` "aaa" text and the labels land on their shapes); the
   character rows map the size the reference stores (offset 18). The
   text block position and the field display text remain open (below).
6. **Every page** (goal 7). The goldens cover every page that draws,
   `<name>-p<N>.png`, with its background page composited behind it as
   Visio draws it: `tdf76829-datetime-format.vsd`'s second page
   (`VHintergrund`) is a background page and has no golden of its own
   (the master's correction).
7. **The twin comparison** (goal 8). `crates/vsd-binary/tests/twins.rs`
   compares `import(<name>.vsd)` with the `.vsdx` reader's document for
   `<name>.vsdx`: pages, shapes joined by Visio id, the cells the
   renderer reads (resolved through the `CellReader`) and the text, per
   page. It skips cleanly while `LARRYGRAM_VSD_TWINS` is unset or the
   twins are absent (like the libvisio oracle), and its contract is
   covered by hand-built tests (10 tests). The twins had not arrived;
   the directory it reads is `LARRYGRAM_VSD_TWINS/vsdx/<name>.vsdx`.

### Deviations

- **The twin runner lives in `crates/render/tests/corpus_vsd.rs`**
  (`the_vsd_imports_match_their_vsdx_twins`). The comparison and its
  contract are `crates/vsd-binary/tests/twins.rs` as the task names
  them, but the `.vsdx` reader is `larrygram-vsdx`, a **same-layer**
  crate `larrygram-vsd-binary` may not depend on — `cargo xtask layers`
  counts dev-dependencies (ADR 0002). The render suite already depends
  on both readers (it hosts the round trip), so it drives the run and
  shares the comparison through a `#[path]` include.
- **The parity harness's rules** (`tests/parity/`, which this task may
  change when a rule is wrong): our side now flattens to one entry per
  drawing shape (a group with no content of its own is not an entry);
  our side read `ShadowPattern`/`ShadowForegnd`, names that do not
  exist (`ShdwPattern`/`ShdwForegnd` are the model's); and the dump
  parser took the first style of a shape, which is the fill pass of the
  generator's two passes, so every shape's stroke read `none`. With the
  rules fixed, `dwg.vsd` reaches 100%.
- **`corpus_vsd.rs`'s round trip** now compares the model in document
  order (page shapes and sub-shapes depth first) rather than arena
  order: the import's arena is stream order, the reader's is encounter
  order, and only the tree order survives the round trip unchanged.

### Open issues

1. **`NoLine`/`NoFill`/`NoShow` do not reach the renderer.** The
   geometry section cells are mapped (both readers map them), but
   `crates/render/src/build.rs` never reads them, so a section that
   says `NoLine` still strokes (`no-bgcolor.vsd`'s box). The fault is
   in the renderer, not the import: the model cells are the same for
   `.vsdx`, so a `.vsdx` file with those cells renders wrong too. A
   fix needs a per-section node in the scene (the sections can differ).
2. **The fill of an open sub-path closes with a diagonal** (the white
   wedges in `no-bgcolor.vsd` and `Visio6PlanWithDimensions.vsd`). Our
   geometry rows keep the file's redundant rows (zero-length segments
   the raw generator drops) and a sub-path whose last point is not its
   first closes with a straight edge when filled. Needs the reference
   PDFs to say whether Visio closes the same way.
3. **Text boxes and field display text** (the largest remaining class:
   143 `text-box.*` mismatches and 59 `text`). The text transform's
   values land on the wrong box, and a field prints its placeholder
   (`Page-1`) where Visio prints its computed value (P8-05's display
   spellings, unchanged).
4. **Quick-style and theme colours** (P8-10's open issue 1) are still
   unmapped; the corpus's visible colours happen to come from the
   colour table and the RGBA values, so the pictures match, but a file
   whose colour comes only from a quick-style index would not.
5. **The document-scope shape, connects, the template marker, the
   creation date, the summary code page and custom properties**
   (P8-07's and P8-10's open issues) are unchanged.
6. **The twins' order and tolerances** are untested against real twins:
   the comparison joins shapes by Visio id and ignores file order on
   purpose (the two formats keep different orders). When the twins
   arrive, `LARRYGRAM_VSD_TWINS=/Volumes/Dara/larrygram/reference/vsd-twins`
   runs it; the PNGs there are cropped to the drawing, so picture
   comparison uses the PDFs.
