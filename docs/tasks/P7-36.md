## Goal

1. **The cells** (rule 8). `style_cells` holds the cell names and the
   values the alpha reads and writes. One function maps a
   connector's cells and its page's cells to our style, and one gives
   the cells a style choice writes:

   | Choice | `ShapeRouteStyle` | `ConLineRouteExt` |
   |---|---|---|
   | Right Angle | 1 | 1 |
   | Straight Lines | 2 | 1 |
   | Curved Lines | 1 | 2 |

   Read order: the shape's cells, then the page's `RouteStyle` and
   `LineRouteExt`, then right angle. Value 16, the directed values and
   unknown values route as right angle. The cells are kept as they
   are; never rewrite a value you only read.
2. **Curved routes.** A curved connector is one `NURBSTo` row from the
   begin point to the end point. The control points are the
   right-angle route's points, of degree min(3, points − 1), on a
   clamped uniform knot vector with weights 1. The row's `X`, `Y`,
   `A`–`D` and `E` follow the reference pages above.
   - Our renderer draws the curve through the existing NURBS path
     (ADR 0022).
   - A two-point route is a straight `LineTo`.
   - The curve leaves each end tangent to its exit direction, because
     the first control segment is the exit stub.
3. **The commands.** `connector.style` (`style`: "right_angle" |
   "straight" | "curved"; optional `page_default`) writes the cells on
   the selected connectors, or on the page sheet when `page_default`
   is true or nothing is selected. It then re-routes the affected
   connectors, in one undoable edit. The style controls listed under
   Owns dispatch it:
   - each shows the current style checked;
   - the context-menu items act on the selection;
   - Design > Connectors with nothing selected sets the page default.
4. **Line jump controls.** The Show Line Jumps, Line Jump Style and
   Add Line Jumps To controls stay disabled in both layouts, with a
   tooltip in our own words saying line jumps aren't available yet.
   Check whether they are disabled today. If not, disable them through
   their enabled-state predicates.
5. **Storage round trip.** `crates/vsdx/tests/connector_roundtrip.rs`
   pins everything a file must carry for our glue to open in Visio:
   - documents our commands build: point glue, dynamic glue, free
     ends, and each style. Each is saved and reopened, and the
     endpoint formulas, triggers, values, style cells, geometry rows
     and `Connect` records come back exactly;
   - every corpus `.vsdx` file with `Connects`
     (`corpus/vsdx/testfile3.vsdx` and
     `tdf136564-WhiteTextBackground.vsdx` today): opened, saved
     unedited and reopened, with every record, endpoint formula and
     trigger unchanged.

   Skip the corpus files with a note when they are absent (corpus
   files are not committed).
