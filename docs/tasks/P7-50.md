## Why

The Opus review of P7-49 (2026-11-04): fix first. Items 1, 4, 5, 6, 7,
9 and 10 are right; undo restores the exact rows, the runner's re-route
and `moves_glue` are gone with no dead code, and the transform-order
fix is correct. Four majors remain:

- **MA1 (regression against main):** `picks` now skips connectors in
  the changed set, and `run_group`/`run_ungroup` pass their members as
  changed. `group_shapes` and `ungroup_shape` rebase only `PinX`/`PinY`,
  so a grouped connector's `BeginX`..`EndY` stay at page values while
  its drawn path is in the group's frame. On main the re-route fixed
  them. A free-ended connector is wrong on main too.
- **MA2:** a connector inside a moved group is re-routed fresh although
  both its targets move with it, so a stored route is lost. Its endpoint
  cells are in the parent's coordinates and do not change in a rigid
  group move; the ADR amendment's reason for re-routing is wrong.
- **MA3:** for a target rotated by an angle that is not a multiple of
  90°, the box check uses the axis-aligned box, which the end's own stub
  always crosses. Every candidate fails, and the fallback (the first
  elbow) can turn 180° back through the shape.
- **MA4 (introduced by P7-49 item 8):** point glue on a group member's
  connection point names the top-level group with the member's row
  index, so the end lands on a different point or falls back to dynamic
  glue.

## Goal

1. **Group and ungroup rebase the endpoint cells** (MA1). In
   `group_shapes` and `ungroup_shape`, a 1-D shape's `BeginX`, `BeginY`,
   `EndX` and `EndY` are mapped into the new parent's coordinates, the
   same way the pins are. Do it in the model so every caller gets it, not
   with a forced re-route (that discards stored routes). Test in the
   model crate and with `assert_cells_match_path` after group, ungroup,
   undo and redo, for a glued connector and a free-ended one.
2. **A rigid group move keeps the route** (MA2). Under
   `Scope::Changed`, re-route a connector only when a glued end's target
   and the connector are on different sides of the move: the target is
   in the moved set or its subtree and the connector is not, or the
   other way round. Correct ADR 0084 rule 5's amendment to say this.
   Tests: a custom 4-point route inside a rigidly moved group survives
   byte for byte, and its undo and redo agree; a connector from outside
   the group to a member is still re-routed.
3. **Rotated targets** (MA3).
   - An end's own stub leg is not tested against its own box, or the
     test uses the target's oriented box in its local frame. Say which in
     the Result.
   - The fallback is the first candidate with no 180° turn; the first
     elbow only when none qualifies.
   - Extend the routing proptest to targets rotated by any angle:
     assert no 180° turn and no crossing of the oriented box's interior.
     Add the review's probe as a unit test: a 2×1 box rotated 30°, each
     of its four candidates routed to a shape below and to its left.
4. **Point glue names the member** (MA4). Map to the top-level ancestor
   only for dynamic glue. Point glue names the shape that owns the
   `Connection` row. Test, real input: a group whose member has a
   connection point; the drag snaps to it, and the glue record names the
   member and that row, and the end sits on that point.
5. **The minors:**
   - the no-fill fallback tests containment in the shape's outline,
     ignoring fill, not the axis-aligned box: a press in the corner area
     of a diamond's box, outside the diamond, does not glue (minor 1);
   - delete the stale `reroute_after_edit` doc comment above `RouteEdit`
     (minor 2);
   - remove the `eprintln!("DBG …")` lines from
     `crates/ui/tests/phase7/connector_landing.rs` (minor 3);
   - make the delete half of
     `a_glue_and_a_delete_restore_a_corpus_route_on_undo` fail without
     the fix, or drop it and say why (minor 4);
   - `RouteEdit` keeps only what `write_path` touches (the geometry
     section and the endpoint cells), not two whole sheets; document that
     `connector.reroute_all` is not an edit (minor 5);
   - ADR 0084 rule 2's amendment: the endpoint cells are in the parent's
     coordinates; the geometry rows are in the connector's own box
     (minor 6);
   - the proptest's generator keeps the normal's axis dominant, or the
     test asserts the route never runs along the shape's own edge
     (minor 7);
   - `candidates.remove(0)` becomes a non-panicking form (minor 8).
