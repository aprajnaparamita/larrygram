## What the user saw (manual test, 2026-10-25)

The UI is not usable yet:

1. **The welcome wizard** opens at an odd aspect ratio and does not
   scale with the window or the display.
2. **The About window ("Why Larrygram")** is oddly laid out and does
   not scale.
3. **Menus and dialogs**: submenus stay popped out; popups and
   controls do not account for the panel's flexible width; elements do
   not pack.
4. **Panel Modern**: clicking New does not create a document.
5. **The New page** shows an empty element above the Blank entry.

Earlier review findings in the same area (P7-10, P7-11, P7-12 and the
master's look at the goldens): illegible Panel Classic menu labels,
disabled icons as dark squares, command-bar labels wrapping one letter
per line, the contextual band hard-coded. P7-14 fixes several of
these; check what is left after it.

## Goal

1. **Look first.** Run the desktop app (`cargo run -p larrygram`) and
   capture screenshots of every surface: the wizard's two pages, the
   About window, the File view and its pages, every Panel Modern tab
   with its groups, the contextual tabs, Panel Classic's menu bar with
   every menu and submenu open, the toolbars, the context menus, the
   Options dialog, the panes, the status bar. Do it at three window
   sizes (1024 x 768, 1440 x 900, 1920 x 1080) and at display scales 1
   and 2, in both layouts. Keep the screenshots local (under
   `/Volumes/Dara/larrygram/reference/ui-review/`), never in git. List
   every defect you see in the Result, by surface, before you fix it.
2. **Fix every defect**, the five above first:
   - the wizard and the About window size from the window (a maximum
     and a minimum size, the content scaling inside, the artwork
     keeping its aspect ratio), and work at both display scales;
   - a submenu opens on hover or click and closes when the pointer or
     focus leaves it, and on Escape; only one submenu chain is open;
   - the command bar adapts to the window width the way the layout's
     group sizing rules describe (large, medium, small, collapsed to a
     group button), and nothing overlaps or spills;
   - controls pack: consistent spacing, alignment and baselines in
     groups, toolbars, menus and dialogs;
   - New on Panel Modern creates a document through the registry, the
     same command as File > New in Panel Classic;
   - the New page has no empty entry.
3. **Tests that would have caught them**, each a kittest test or a
   golden:
   - at each of the three window sizes: no two controls overlap, every
     control lies inside its parent, no label wraps inside a word;
   - after an open-then-leave sequence, no popup is left open;
   - the wizard's and the About window's rectangles at both scales;
   - New creates a document (the command and the resulting state).
   Regenerate the goldens that change and look at each one.
4. **Report**: a before and after table per surface in the Result, and
   any defect left with its cause.

## Result

### Step 1: the defects found (before any fix)

How the surfaces were captured: the desktop app (`cargo run -p
larrygram`) at 1024 x 768 and 1440 x 900 on the Retina display (scale
2), launch states only (the window was seeded through the app's
storage file; the 1920 x 1080 window does not fit this display), and
a kittest sweep (`crates/ui/tests/phase7/review.rs`, ignored unless
`LARRYGRAM_UI_REVIEW_DIR` is set) that renders every surface of both
layouts at the three window sizes and at scales 1 and 2 through the
same widgets: 340 images under
`/Volumes/Dara/larrygram/reference/ui-review/before/`. Scale 2
changes only the sharpness; every layout defect below shows at both
scales.

**Welcome wizard (both pages, both layouts)**
- W1. The window takes no size from the display: about 340 x 420 pt
  at every window size, a tall narrow box with the art shrunk to a
  thumbnail and half the box empty below the buttons. (User defect 1.)
- W2. The footer buttons float in the middle of the box, not at its
  bottom edge.
- W3. The layout page shows the two pictures at thumbnail size with
  no heading; the box size changes nothing when the window grows.

**About window**
- A1. Opens at the top-left corner of the window, not centred, at a
  fixed small size; the photo is a thumbnail and the text column is
  narrow at every window size. (User defect 2.)
- A2. The same window opened from the wizard draws on top of the
  wizard at its own unrelated size.

**Options dialog**
- O1. Opens at the top-left corner, over the File button and the QAT;
  a second "Options" heading repeats the title bar.

**Menus and popups (user defect 3)**
- M1. Panel Classic menu bar, context menus and toolbar popups: an
  open submenu stays open when the pointer leaves the menus; it only
  closes on a click or when another row is hovered.
- M2. Panel Modern menus (the split and dropdown menus): the egui
  submenu stays open when the pointer leaves every menu.
- M3. Panel Classic floating toolbars (Drawing, Ink, Picture) open as
  windows the width of the whole frame with a vertical strip of
  buttons in their left edge; the windows overlap each other.

**Panel Modern command bar**
- C1. No width adaptation: at 1024 x 768 the Home tab shows
  Clipboard, Font and half of Paragraph; at 1440 x 900 Shape Styles
  and everything after Tools are cut off; the ^ collapse button draws
  over the last visible group. (User defect 3.)
- C2. Large split buttons (Paste, Bring to Front, Send to Back, Line)
  draw their label twice: once under the icon, clipped, and once in
  the arrow part.
- C3. The Font Size combo's label wraps to two lines ("Font / Size").
- C4. A 30 px empty band between the tab strip and the groups.
- C5. The contextual tab-set caption ("Picture Tools") draws inside
  the tab strip over its tab's label ("FORMAT").
- C6. The Tools group shows an empty split button (no label, no icon).
- C7. Group launchers are wide pill buttons that overlap the caption
  text in narrow groups (Page Setup, Font).

**Panel Modern File view**
- F1. Clicking New does not create a document: the Blank Drawing tile
  runs `file.new.blank.drawing`, a stub, so it is disabled. (User
  defect 4.)
- F2. The New page shows a disabled "(none)" tile above Blank Drawing
  (the empty template list's placeholder). (User defect 5.)
- F3. Page headings are body-size text; the pages draw flush against
  the nav column; the back arrow is a 12 px glyph.

**Status bar (Panel Modern)**
- S1. The status message ("Selected 0 shapes", "About Larrygram") and
  the "PAGE n OF m" text draw under the right-hand buttons at 1024 x
  768: the text and the Full Screen button overlap.
- S2. The status-bar buttons draw white text on a pale fill, unreadable
  whether enabled or not.
- S3. With no document it reads "PAGE 0 OF 1".

**Panes and canvas**
- P1. Shapes window: the stencil thumbnails are clipped at the top
  (Ellipse, Arc, Terminator lose their top edge).
- P2. Shapes window (Panel Classic): 440 px wide at 1024 x 768, more
  than 40% of the window.
- P3. Shape Data pane: the value column is clipped at the right edge
  at every size.
- P4. The canvas draws the grid only on the off-page area, and the
  off-page area is white like the page.
- P5. Panel Classic with no document (desktop app): a black band
  between the Shapes window and the drawing area.

**Panel Classic toolbars**
- T1. At 1024 x 768 the Standard and Formatting toolbars wrap into
  four rows and the last button is cut in half at the right edge.

**Found while fixing**
- X1. Every Panel Modern menu with a submenu (Line, Effects, Position,
  Backgrounds, Check Diagram) and the Editing group's Select draw
  disabled: their own command is a stub, which disabled the whole
  menu.
- X2. A context menu the shell opens (right-click on a page tab, the
  Shapes window, the panes, the canvas) closes in the frame it opens
  and has no position: no right-click menu ever showed.
- X3. Split arrows in Panel Classic toolbars draw 30 px wide.

### Tests that would have caught them

- `tests/phase7/fit.rs`: at 1024 x 768, 1440 x 900 and 1920 x 1080,
  scales 1 and 2, both layouts: no two chrome controls (labels
  included) overlap, every chrome control lies inside the window, and
  no painted label wraps inside a word. The checks have their own
  tests (`mid_word_wraps` flags a split word and accepts a wrap at a
  space).
- `tests/phase7/popups.rs`: open-then-leave sequences at the three
  sizes leave no popup open (Panel Classic File > New, Panel Modern
  Paste menu); a collapsed group opens and closes; right-clicking the
  page tab draws its menu at the pointer in both layouts; New in the
  File view creates a document through `file.new`, and the Classic
  File > New menu names the same command.
- `tests/phase7/windows.rs`: the wizard's and the About window's
  rects at the three sizes and both scales (centred, inside, the
  `window_size` rule, the art's aspect, the footer on the bottom edge,
  the same size in points at both scales, both wizard pages one size).
- Unit tests: `window_size` (the rule, the footer split, the aspect
  fit, the text scale); the Panel Classic leave rule for the menu bar
  and context menus, with the keyboard exemption; the Panel Modern
  leave rule with a fixture submenu; `menu_reachable`; the preview
  margin.
- The review sweep itself (`tests/phase7/review.rs`, ignored) stays
  for the next review.
- Every Phase 7 golden was regenerated and looked at.

### Deviations

- No layout data changed. The command bar's reduction order is a
  fixed rule in code (right to left, one stage at a time, the
  stages above), documented in `docs/ui/panel-modern.md` "Narrow
  windows"; the layout has no per-group sizing fields to read.
- One theme value changed: Panel Modern `canvas.background` from
  `#fbfbfb` to `#e6e6e6` (the off-page area was indistinguishable
  from the page).
- The contextual tab-set name sits inside the tab, in front of its
  label, not in a caption row above it (the 25 px strip has no room);
  the guide says so.
- The two Phase 7 Classic menu tests that pinned "a submenu stays
  open when the pointer leaves" now open the submenu from the
  keyboard (the new rule closes a pointer-opened submenu on leave).
- The ADR 0065 sweep's expectation for a dropdown whose own command
  is a stub became "opens its menu" when an entry of the menu runs.
- The tab-strip and group widgets draw in child uis: `ui.put` in the
  parent advanced its cursor by the item spacing per call.

### Open issues

- **`file.new.blank.drawing` is a stub** (and `.metric`, `.us`), so
  Panel Classic's File > New > New Drawing (Ctrl+N row) and its two
  unit variants draw disabled. Panel Modern's New now runs `file.new`,
  which Classic's New Drawing from Template... also runs. For
  `crates/commands`: register the three ids over `file::run_new` (the
  units can follow later), or for the layout data owner: point New
  Drawing at `file.new`.
- **No committed Panel Modern submenu has a runnable entry yet**: the
  children of Line, Effects, Position, Backgrounds and Check Diagram
  are stubs, so those menus stay disabled under the new reachability
  rule. The submenu leave rule is pinned with a fixture submenu
  (`panel_modern::tests`); the shell-level test uses the Paste menu.
- **Status-bar buttons are text.** Visio draws icons; the icon set
  has keys for them, but on the blue bar they need a light tint token
  (`status_bar.icon`) in the theme and a tinted icon atom.
- **The wizard's Welcome text sits at the top of its column** while
  the art is centred vertically; cosmetic, left as is.
- **Panel 2016 and Panel 365** (the Fluent-data layouts) were not part
  of this review; they share the Panel Modern widgets, so the
  command-bar and menu fixes apply to them, but their chrome was not
  captured.
- **Kittest note for test authors:** `node.click()` (an accesskit
  click) does not open an egui `Popup::menu`; the popup tests click
  with pointer events (`review::click_at`).
- `larrygram-commands` prints a dead-code warning for
  `default_save_name` on the wasm target (`crates/commands/src/builtins/file.rs:238`).
