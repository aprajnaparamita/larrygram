## Goal

1. **Moving a 1-D shape** (review 1; ADR 0084 rule 1, amended).
   `shape.move` and the pointer body drag translate the four endpoint
   cells and re-write the path. Glued ends become free in the same
   edit, except an end whose target moves in the same edit. Align and
   distribute skip 1-D shapes. Resize, rotate and flip refuse a 1-D
   shape with a status message and push no undo entry. No command
   reports success for an edit that changed nothing.
2. **Copying and dropping** (review 2). Duplicate and paste offset the
   endpoint cells with the pin. An end glued to a shape copied in the
   same operation re-glues to the copy, formula and record renumbered
   together; any other glued end becomes free at its stored point. A
   drop of the Connector master from the Shapes window places begin
   and end from the drop point and the master's begin-to-end vector.
   `instantiate_master` stops writing literals over the master's
   GUARD pin and size formulas for a 1-D master.
3. **Group delete** (review 3). Deleting, or cutting, a group clears
   the glue formulas of every connector glued to any shape in the
   removed subtree. Test it.
4. **The delete's small fixes** (review 5 to 7). A dangling record is
   skipped (`continue`), not a reason to clear nothing. A freed end
   keeps its stored point (`connector_end`), not a re-resolved one.
   Remove the wrong `edit.cut` open issue from P7-31's Result.
5. **Routability** (review 8; ADR 0084 rule 1, amended). Add
   `Document::is_routable(shape)`: `ObjType` has `&H2` and not `&H4`.
   Only routable shapes get the right-angle default, the style cells
   and a routed path; any other glued 1-D shape stays a straight line
   from begin to end. A pin or size cell with a formula keeps it
   through a path write. Count, in the local `corpus/vsdx` files,
   the 1-D shapes that are a `FromSheet` and are not routable, and
   put the count in the Result.
6. **Review 4 and the nits.** Remove the dead walk in
   `connector_visio_ids`. Fix the `write_connector_ends` doc comment.
   `end_glue_of` returns an error for an out-of-range index instead of
   row 0. The path write refreshes `Angle` through its formula as it
   does the pin.
