## Goal

1. **Moving a 1-D shape** (review 1; ADR 0084 rule 1, amended).
   `shape.move` and the pointer body drag translate the four endpoint
   cells and re-write the path. Glued ends become free in the same
   edit, except an end whose target moves in the same edit. Align and
   distribute skip 1-D shapes. Resize, rotate and flip refuse a 1-D
   shape with a status message and push no undo entry. No command
   reports success for an edit that changed nothing.
2. **Copying and dropping** (review 2). Duplicate and paste offset the
   endpoint cells with the pin. An end glued to a shape copied in the
   same operation re-glues to the copy, formula and record renumbered
   together; any other glued end becomes free at its stored point. A
   drop of the Connector master from the Shapes window places begin
   and end from the drop point and the master's begin-to-end vector.
   `instantiate_master` stops writing literals over the master's
   GUARD pin and size formulas for a 1-D master.
3. **Group delete** (review 3). Deleting, or cutting, a group clears
   the glue formulas of every connector glued to any shape in the
   removed subtree. Test it.
4. **The delete's small fixes** (review 5 to 7). A dangling record is
   skipped (`continue`), not a reason to clear nothing. A freed end
   keeps its stored point (`connector_end`), not a re-resolved one.
   Remove the wrong `edit.cut` open issue from P7-31's Result.
5. **Routability** (review 8; ADR 0084 rule 1, amended). Add
   `Document::is_routable(shape)`: `ObjType` has `&H2` and not `&H4`.
   Only routable shapes get the right-angle default, the style cells
   and a routed path; any other glued 1-D shape stays a straight line
   from begin to end. A pin or size cell with a formula keeps it
   through a path write. Count, in the local `corpus/vsdx` files,
   the 1-D shapes that are a `FromSheet` and are not routable, and
   put the count in the Result.
6. **Review 4 and the nits.** Remove the dead walk in
   `connector_visio_ids`. Fix the `write_connector_ends` doc comment.
   `end_glue_of` returns an error for an out-of-range index instead of
   row 0. The path write refreshes `Angle` through its formula as it
   does the pin.

**What changed**

- **Item 1, moves** (review 1). A new `OneDMoveEdit` in
  `builtins/connector.rs` moves 1-D shapes by their endpoint cells:
  `translate_one_d` shifts the ends and re-writes the drawn route (the
  bends are kept) in the parent's space, or, for formula-driven
  geometry, moves only the ends and the box
  (`Document::write_connector_box`, new). An end whose target is not
  moving in the same command comes loose in the same edit: its formula
  becomes a literal, its trigger names its own sheet, and its record
  goes. One undo restores the sheet and the page's records exactly.
  `shape.move` (and so the pointer body drag) and `shape.set.pin` use
  it. A new helper, `edit::without_one_d`, makes align and distribute
  skip 1-D shapes. Resize, rotate (`shape.rotate` and both
  `arrange.rotate90`), flip and the `shape.set.width`/`height`/`angle`
  fields refuse them with "… does not apply to a connector or a line;
  drag its ends instead" and push no undo step. With a mixed
  selection, the 2-D shapes are edited and each 1-D shape gets a
  warning line. A path edit's undo now restores the whole sheet, not
  only its sections. `pointer.rs` needed no change.
- **Item 2, copies and drops** (review 2). `paste_content`
  (`clipboard.rs`, used by paste, paste-in-place and duplicate) moves
  a 1-D root by its ends with the offset or the pointer. Before the
  records are added, `rebind_copied_glue` rewrites each copied 1-D
  shape's endpoint formulas. An end whose record was copied re-glues
  to the copy (formula, trigger and record renumbered together). Every
  other end comes loose at its stored point.
  `Document::instantiate_master` and `stencils::instantiate` give a
  1-D master's instance only its four endpoint cells: the drop point
  is the midpoint, the master's begin-to-end vector gives the
  direction (scaled to a requested width), and a path write fills the
  pin and size cells through the master's GUARD formulas. `insert.rs`
  skips the constant-geometry rescale for a 1-D drop.
- **Item 3, group delete** (review 3). `GlueClearEdit::stage` and the
  delete's glue warnings walk every removed subtree
  (`subtree_visio_ids`), so deleting or cutting a group frees every
  end glued to one of its members, in the delete's own edit.
- **Item 4, the delete fixes** (reviews 5 to 7). A dangling record (no
  connector, or a legacy connector) is skipped and the other ends are
  still cleared. A freed end keeps its stored point (the endpoint
  cells). The wrong `edit.cut` open issue in P7-31's Result is
  replaced by a note.
- **Item 5, routability** (review 8). `Document::is_routable`: the
  `ObjType` value (own or inherited) is a whole number with `&H2` set
  and `&H4` clear. `route_style_for` routes a 1-D shape that is not
  routable as a straight line from begin to end, and `connector.style`
  skips it. A path write keeps every pin, size and `Angle` formula
  (own or inherited) and caches the value it computes. It handles the
  connector-box family (`Width` = `EndX-BeginX`) and the aligned-line
  family (an `ATAN2` `Angle`: `Width` is the length, `Angle` the
  direction). `Height` keeps its value unless its formula reads the
  ends (`GUARD(EndY-BeginY)`). Only literal cells get literals; a
  missing cell gets the documented formula.
- **Item 6, the nits** (review 4 and 9). The dead walk in
  `connector_visio_ids` is gone. The `write_connector_ends` doc comment
  says what the code does. `end_glue_of` returns an error for a row
  index beyond `u32` instead of row 0. The path write refreshes
  `Angle` with the pin, and `Angle` is in the path edit's dirty set.
